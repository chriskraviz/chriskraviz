## Hi there 👋
# Christian Giersch

**AI Solution Architect & Data Engineer**

I build practical AI tools, data applications and automations that make recurring work easier to understand and easier to improve.

My focus is on turning complex technical possibilities into small, useful applications with clear data paths, human review and room to grow.

## Selected projects

### [Griffel](https://github.com/chriskraviz/griffel)

A native macOS app for dictation, text revision and thought capture. Griffel is a further development of the open-source project Blitztext, with a strong focus on local processing and keeping confidential content on the device.

### AI Council

A working prototype for structured decision support. Several AI perspectives examine a question, challenge each other's arguments and produce a reasoned recommendation. The final decision stays with the human.

## Areas of focus

- AI and data applications
- Collaboration between specialised AI agents
- Workflow automation
- Data and cloud architecture
- Human review, privacy and extensibility

## About

I am based in the Stuttgart region, Germany. Alongside my professional work, I develop and document small tools that test ideas in practice.
